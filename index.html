Statement of purpose · Rev B
Fig. 02 · Line fieldThe lines thicken where you are, the way a design thickens where a person meets it.

What I was put here to do is to design: to shape things, systems and experiences so people and the planet can flourish together.

Guiding thesis. Before something can be manufactured, programmed, communicated or experienced, it must first be imagined and designed.

03 · Five giftsModules 01 to 05 · Exploded view

Five gifts.

One instrument, five parts. Scroll to take it apart. Point at a part to read it.

Gift 01

Empathy

Like stepping into someone else’s shoes before you draw the map.

Understanding another person’s experience before proposing anything for them.

Gift 02

Visual Imagination

Like a sketchbook that holds the city before anyone has built it.

Turning an idea from wireframe to blueprint to solid form.

Gift 03

Systems Thinking

Like seeing the whole web while others only see one thread.

Seeing how each component changes the whole.

Gift 04

Craft

Like a watchmaker who knows the smallest gear sets the hour.

Precision, iteration and respect for detail.

Gift 05

Clear Communication

Like a lens: it adds no light, it brings the picture into focus.

Making complexity understandable to the person receiving it.
